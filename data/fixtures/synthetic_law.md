## Chapter 1 — Definitions

### Article 1 — Scope

This Law governs all financial procedures of Testland Government entities.

### Article 2 — Definitions

For the purposes of this Law:

- **Fiscal Authority:** The central body responsible for issuing budgetary guidelines.
- **Budget Circular:** The official document issued annually to initiate budget preparation.
- **Supreme Fiscal Committee (SFC):** The committee responsible for endorsing expenditure ceilings.
- **Budget Committee:** An internal committee formed by each entity to manage budget preparation.
- **Core Budgeting Principles:** The four principles of Comprehensiveness, Universality, Annuality, and Unity.
- **Prescribed Forms:** Documentation templates approved by the Director General.
- **Director General:** Head of the Fiscal Authority.

---

## Chapter 2 — Budget Preparation

### Article 3 — General Budget Preparation Rules

All government entities shall prepare annual budgets in accordance with the Core Budgeting Principles.

### Article 4 — Fiscal Authority Functions

The Fiscal Authority shall: establish fiscal frameworks; issue guidelines; monitor compliance; and advise entities on budgetary obligations.

### Article 5 — Budget Preparation Circular

The Fiscal Authority shall issue the Budget Preparation Circular by April 30 of each year. The circular must receive prior endorsement from the Supreme Fiscal Committee (SFC) before issuance. The circular shall specify: expenditure ceilings; submission deadlines; and applicable legal constraints.

### Article 6 — Internal Budget Preparation Committees

Every government entity shall establish an internal Budget Preparation Committee within 30 days of receiving the Budget Circular. The committee shall: coordinate budget submissions; ensure compliance with the Circular; and report to senior management.

### Article 7 — Core Budgeting Principles

All budgets shall adhere to the following principles:
1. **Comprehensiveness:** All revenues and expenditures must be included.
2. **Universality:** No netting of revenues against expenditures.
3. **Annuality:** Budget covers exactly one fiscal year.
4. **Unity:** A single consolidated budget document.

### Article 8 — Budget Submission Templates

All entities shall use templates prescribed by the Director General for budget submissions. Templates must capture: revenue estimates; expenditure estimates; performance targets; and legal constraints.

### Article 9 — Calendar and Milestones

The Fiscal Authority shall publish the annual budget calendar specifying: key milestone dates; inter-agency coordination deadlines; and approval submission dates.

### Article 10 — Legal and Policy Constraints

All budget submissions must explicitly outline the applicable legal and policy constraints under which each department operates.

## Chapter 3 — Reporting and Audit

### Article 11 — Accounting Records

Entities shall maintain accounting records in forms prescribed by the Director General.

### Article 12 — Internal Audit

Each entity shall establish an internal audit function reporting directly to senior management.

### Article 13 — External Audit

The Supreme Audit Institution shall audit all government entities annually.

### Article 14 — Reporting Obligations

Entities shall submit quarterly financial reports to the Fiscal Authority within 15 days of the end of each quarter.

### Article 15 — Compliance Monitoring

The Fiscal Authority shall monitor compliance with this Law and report violations to the SFC within 30 days of discovery.
