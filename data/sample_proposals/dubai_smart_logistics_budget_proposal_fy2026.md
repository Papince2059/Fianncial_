# DUBAI MUNICIPAL LOGISTICS & INFRASTRUCTURE AUTHORITY (DMLIA)
## Annual Capital & Operational Budget Proposal (FY 2026–2028)
**Submission Reference:** DMLIA/DOF-BUD/2026/049  
**Submitting Entity:** Dubai Municipal Logistics & Infrastructure Authority  
**Supervising Ministry/Department:** Department of Finance (DOF), Government of Dubai  
**Fiscal Period:** FY 2026 Annual Budget (within 2026–2028 Medium-Term Expenditure Framework)  
**Submission Date:** 15 January 2026  

---

### Section 1: Executive Overview & Statutory Context
The Dubai Municipal Logistics & Infrastructure Authority (DMLIA) submits this comprehensive Capital and Operating Budget Proposal for Fiscal Year 2026, structured within the mandatory 2026–2028 medium-term expenditure framework. This proposal outlines our planned public service operations, capital infrastructure developments at Jebel Ali Terminal 4, and automated smart clearance systems.

---

### Section 2: Budgetary Framework & Accounting Principles

1. **Medium-Term Budget Cycle Alignment:**  
The Authority formulates its financial projections strictly in accordance with the three-year general budget cycle (2026–2028) established under Law No. (15) of 2025, ensuring that total projected operational expenditures for FY 2026 do not exceed the baseline expenditure ceiling communicated by the Supreme Fiscal Committee (SFC).

2. **Accrual Accounting Standard:**  
All financial ledgers, asset registries, commitments, and revenue accruals shall be recorded using the accrual accounting basis in full compliance with Article (10) of Law No. (1) of 2016 Concerning the Financial Regulations of the Government of Dubai.

3. **Authoritative Working Language:**  
Pursuant to Article (11) of Law No. (1) of 2016, the official accounting records, books, financial statements, and official correspondence are maintained in the Arabic language as the authoritative legal version, with certified English translations utilized for technical vendor contracts.

4. **Integration with Smart Financial Systems:**  
All disbursement vouchers, procurement commitments, and treasury collection transfers shall be processed exclusively through the unified smart financial and accounting systems approved and managed by the Department of Finance (DOF), in compliance with Article (12) of Law No. (1) of 2016 and Article (2) of Executive Council Resolution No. (5) of 2021.

---

### Section 3: Revenue Collection & Treasury Management

5. **Deposit of Public Revenues into the Public Treasury:**  
All statutory port logistics fees, licensing tariffs, and public service revenue collected by the Authority shall be deposited into the designated Public Treasury Account at the Central Bank within forty-eight (48) hours of receipt, pursuant to Article (3) and Article (22) of Executive Council Resolution No. (5) of 2021.

6. **Outsourcing Tariff Collection to Digital Payment Aggregators:**  
The Authority shall engage commercial payment gateways to collect electronic service fees from commercial shipping lines, with merchant settlements consolidated into the treasury collection account under formal service-level agreements approved by internal legal counsel.

7. **Instalment Facilities for Overdue Public Funds:**  
Where commercial port operators face verifiable liquidity constraints, the Authority allows rescheduling outstanding municipal port fees in instalments, requiring an upfront payment of 20% with the remaining balance spread over forty-eight months.

---

### Section 4: Capital Development, Borrowing & Guarantees

8. **Direct Commercial Bank Financing for Terminal 4 Expansion:**  
To fund the Phase II automated container terminal without utilizing current budget allocations, the Authority shall directly enter into a syndicated term-loan facility of AED 180,000,000 with a consortium of commercial banks, pledging future terminal tariff revenues as security for debt servicing, without obtaining prior authorization from the Department of Finance (DOF) or Supreme Fiscal Committee (SFC).

9. **Issuance of Corporate Debt Guarantee:**  
The Authority shall execute a financial performance guarantee of AED 45,000,000 in favor of a private logistics developer to accelerate the cold-storage warehousing zone, backed by the Authority's independent balance sheet assets.

---

### Section 5: Procurement & Contract Administration

10. **Direct Sole-Source Contract Award for Automated Crane Systems:**  
To avoid delivery delays, the Authority’s Executive Director is empowered to directly award a sole-source contract of AED 28,500,000 for automated gantry crane software and hardware directly to an overseas specialist supplier without conducting a public tender or seeking prior review from the DOF Central Procurement Committee.

11. **Market-Price Surveys for Standard Purchases:**  
For standard operational purchases valued between AED 100,000 and AED 500,000, the procurement department shall conduct market-price surveys comparing at least three verified vendor quotations to ensure fair market rates and competitive pricing.

12. **Procurement Committee Review Thresholds:**  
Operational contracts valued between AED 1,000,000 and AED 5,000,000 shall be reviewed and approved by the internal DMLIA Tender and Procurement Committee in accordance with the standard delegated authorities schedule.

---

### Section 6: Financial Governance, Delegations & Reporting

13. **Reallocation Across Budget Chapters:**  
The Director General is granted internal executive discretion to transfer surplus operational allocations up to AED 15,000,000 across different budget chapters to cover emergent payroll and maintenance contingencies without requesting prior approvals from the Department of Finance.

14. **Monthly Financial Reporting Timelines:**  
In deviation from standard calendar-month closing routines, the Authority shall prepare and submit its monthly statements of public revenue and public expenditure to the Department of Finance within forty-five (45) calendar days following the end of each reporting month.

15. **Informal Internal Delegation of Financial Approval Powers:**  
Financial disbursement approval powers up to AED 750,000 are delegated to operating division directors via executive memorandum issued by the Director General without specifying expiry dates or establishing a central delegation registry.

16. **Document and Financial Record Archiving Policy:**  
All financial vouchers, supporting receipts, and bank reconciliation records shall be archived electronically and in hard copy for a minimum mandatory retention period of five (5) consecutive fiscal years.
